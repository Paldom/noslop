Q2 results stand as a testament to disciplined execution in today's
ever-evolving retail landscape. Revenue reached $48.7M, up 12.3% year over
year, while gross margin expanded 140 basis points to 61.2%. It's not just
growth, it's quality of growth, underscoring the importance of mix.

Store count grew from 118 to 127, with comparable sales up 4.1%. E-commerce
delved deeper into wallet share, showcasing pivotal gains: orders rose 18%,
average basket hit $73.40, and returns fell to 6.8%. These figures highlight
the significance of our intricate fulfillment upgrades, ensuring seamless
delivery within 2.1 days on average and fostering commendable satisfaction
scores of 4.6 out of 5.

Guidance for Q3: revenue between $51M and $53M, capex of $4.2M, and 3 to 5
new locations. Not only does the balance sheet remain strong at $19.8M cash,
but leverage also sits at 1.4x EBITDA, reflecting vibrant, meticulous, and
enduring financial stewardship, paving the way for a transformative second
half and marking a significant milestone in our multifaceted journey.
