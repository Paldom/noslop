ok so the sourdough thing finally clicked for me and of course it was the
dumbest possible fix. my starter was fine. my flour was fine. my problem was
that our kitchen sits at like 17 degrees in winter and every guide assumes
you live in a warm apartment.

moved the jar on top of the fridge where the compressor keeps it warm and
boom, doubled in five hours instead of maybe-kinda-rising overnight.

the loaf from tuesday actually had ears. my partner heard me yell from the
other room and thought something was wrong lol.

anyway if your bread is dense and sad and you've been blaming your starter,
go stick a thermometer where the jar lives. bet you it's cold. the bacteria
don't care about your feelings, they care about temperature.

make a brick. probably a brick honestly.
