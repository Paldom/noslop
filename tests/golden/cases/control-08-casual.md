quick update on the van build since people keep asking. electrical is DONE.
took three weekends, two blown fuses, and one very patient friend who does
this for a living.

we went with a 200Ah battery, a 2000W inverter, and roof solar. total damage
was around $1,900 which hurt but the alternative was doing it twice.

things nobody tells you: crimping is a skill and your first ten crimps are
garbage. buy double the lugs you think you need. also label every wire the
moment you cut it, not later. later is a lie.

the fridge runs, the lights work, and the diesel heater no longer scares me
when it clicks. calling that a win.

insulation is next month. wool vs foam board debate is raging in our house.
wool breathes and doesn't squeak, foam is cheaper and everyone on the
forums swears by it. we'll probably do wool and complain about the price
the whole time. that's the pattern here.
